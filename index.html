01 / Introduction

About me

I'm a high school senior in Colorado with a fascination for mathematical ideas and their applications to problems that matter to people.

My work spans pure and applied mathematics, from differential equations on fractals to Markov-chain models of educational achievement and legislative processes. I'm especially interested in how rigorous analysis can help inform more effective decisions in education and government.

Outside research, I co-founded Equity Learning to make tutoring and enrichment more accessible, organized TEDx events, and work with Colorado youth councils on issues including workforce development and mental-health access.

Research interests

Applied mathematics, stochastic processes, mathematical modeling, education policy, and public-sector decision-making.

What motivates me

Connecting analytical rigor with the experiences and needs of the communities affected by policy decisions.

Research

I enjoy moving between abstract mathematics and data-driven questions about the systems around us.

01

Educational Achievement Mobility

Using Markov chains and longitudinal student data to study transitions in academic achievement, differences across socioeconomic groups, and potential intervention points.

02

Bottlenecks and Committee Filtering: A Markov Chain Analysis of Bill Progression in the Colorado General Assembly

Modeling the progression of bills through state legislatures to examine procedural bottlenecks and how patterns of legislative success vary across sessions.
